## Project Overview
The UIC WiCS site serves as the primary touchpoint for over 300 members. This redesign focused on accessibility and community engagement, moving from a static site to a dynamic CMS-driven platform.

## Key Improvements
- **Event Integration:** A real-time calendar syncing with Google Calendar to display upcoming workshops and socials.
- **Member Spotlights:** A dynamic section managed via Contentful to highlight achievements within the community.
- **Glassmorphism UI:** A modern aesthetic reflecting the high-tech focus of the organization.

## Outcomes
The new site saw a 40% increase in mobile traffic and simplified the process for board members to update content without touching the codebase.
