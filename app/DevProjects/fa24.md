## The Vision
Navigating the CS curriculum can be daunting. **Course Bits** was designed to bridge the gap between official course descriptions and the actual student experience. 

## Features
- **Difficulty Heatmaps:** Visualizes the workload intensity throughout the semester based on past student data.
- **Peer Recommendations:** Verified student reviews focusing on study tips and prerequisite readiness.
- **Professor Archives:** Aggregated data on teaching styles and grading distributions.

## Technical Challenge
Implementing a robust authentication system using **Supabase Auth** to ensure only UIC students can leave reviews, while maintaining anonymity to encourage honest feedback.
